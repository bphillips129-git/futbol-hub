Match Event Log

Two different kinds of thing get logged here, kept separate on purpose: ball actions (what the ball-carrier actually did - a shot and its outcome, or a pass, cross, header, throw-in, dribble, defensive tackle/block) and restarts (a kickoff/reset, goal kick, corner, throw-in, etc). A restart is a clue, not proof, that a shot happened: a goal kick only tells you the ball went out over the endline last touched by an offensive player - a missed shot causes that, but so does an errant pass or a turnover. A corner only tells you the last touch was a defensive player's - a save or a block causes that, but so does a defender clearing a cross that was never really a shot. Log each at its own timestamp; watch footage in your own player (or a clip sent to you) and record what you see here.

The ball-action categories mirror the taxonomy used by SoccerNet's Ball Action Spotting benchmark (Pass, Drive, Header, Cross, Throw-in, Shot, Block, Tackle) rather than a one-off list, specifically so labels collected here can double as training data for an action-type classifier: the detector currently flags anything moving fast near the goal as a shot candidate with no notion of "that was actually a pass," so most flagged candidates will turn out to be something else - pick whatever it actually was, not just correct/incorrect.
